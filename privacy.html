# SMS Messaging Privacy Policy **Last Updated: August 10, 2026** This Privacy Policy describes how mobile phone numbers and related information are handled when SMS messaging is used for authentication, verification, and software development/testing purposes. ## Information Collected A mobile phone number may be provided by a user or associated with an account for the purpose of receiving authentication or verification messages. SMS messaging may be used to deliver one-time verification codes and other transactional security messages initiated by the user. ## Use of Mobile Information Mobile phone numbers are used solely for authentication, account verification, security notifications, and related software development and testing activities. Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent information, and opt-in data will **not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes**. Information may be provided to telecommunications providers and messaging service providers only as necessary to deliver requested SMS messages. ## SMS Consent Users consent to receive SMS messages by voluntarily selecting SMS as a verification method or otherwise requesting an SMS verification message. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services. ## Message Frequency Message frequency varies based on user activity. Messages are generally sent only when a user initiates an authentication, verification, or related transactional process. ## Message and Data Rates **Message and data rates may apply.** Charges are determined by the user's mobile carrier and service plan. ## Data Security Reasonable measures are used to protect mobile phone numbers and related information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse. ## Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in messaging practices, technology, or applicable requirements. Updates will be posted on this page. ## Contact Questions regarding this privacy policy or SMS messaging practices may be directed to the contact information associated with this website.
