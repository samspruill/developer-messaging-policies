Spruill Software Solutions SMS Messaging Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Spruill Software Solutions handles mobile phone numbers and related information when SMS messaging is used for authentication, verification, security, and related software development and testing activities.

Information Collected

Spruill Software Solutions may collect or receive a mobile phone number when a user provides or confirms that number for the purpose of receiving authentication, verification, or other transactional security messages.

SMS messaging may be used to deliver one-time verification codes and related security notifications initiated by the user.

Use of Mobile Information

Spruill Software Solutions uses mobile phone numbers solely for authentication, account verification, security notifications, and related software development and testing purposes.

Spruill Software Solutions does not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS consent information, or opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information may be provided to telecommunications providers, messaging providers, and other service providers only when necessary to deliver requested SMS messages or support related technical and security services.

SMS Consent

Users consent to receive SMS messages from Spruill Software Solutions by providing or confirming their mobile phone number, voluntarily selecting SMS as an authentication or verification method, and explicitly requesting a verification code.

SMS messages are transactional and user-initiated. They are not used for unsolicited marketing or promotional communications.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on user activity. Messages are generally sent only when a user initiates an authentication, verification, or related security process.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Charges are determined by the user's mobile carrier and wireless service plan.

Opt-Out and Help

Where supported, users may reply STOP to opt out of receiving SMS messages and HELP for assistance.

Users who opt out of SMS messaging may need to select another available authentication or verification method.

Data Security

Spruill Software Solutions uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect mobile phone numbers and related information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

Data Sharing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent information, and opt-in data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to provide SMS delivery, telecommunications, security, hosting, or related technical services.

Changes to This Policy

Spruill Software Solutions may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in messaging practices, technology, or applicable requirements. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the SMS messaging practices of Spruill Software Solutions may be directed to the contact information associated with this website.
