Spruill Software Solutions SMS Authentication Opt-In

Authentication, verification, and one-time passcode messaging

Users may choose SMS as an authentication or account verification method within applications that use Spruill Software Solutions messaging services.

SMS messages are sent only after the user provides or confirms a mobile phone number, voluntarily selects SMS verification, and explicitly requests a one-time verification code.

How Users Opt In

An end-user provides or confirms a mobile phone number and voluntarily selects SMS as the desired authentication or verification method.

Before requesting a verification code, the user is informed that selecting SMS authorizes Spruill Software Solutions to send transactional authentication and verification messages to the provided mobile number.

SMS messages are user-initiated and are not used for unsolicited marketing or promotional communications.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on user activity. Messages are generally sent only when the user initiates an authentication, account verification, or related security process.

Opt-Out and Help

Users may reply STOP to opt out of receiving SMS messages and HELP for assistance, where supported.

Users who opt out of SMS messaging may need to select another available authentication or verification method.

Where supported, users may reply START to resume receiving SMS messages after previously opting out.

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent information, and opt-in data are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
